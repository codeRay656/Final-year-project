📌 Project OverviewThis repository contains the computational models, data, and research findings for optimizing broiler chicken feed formulation in the poultry industry. Traditional feed formulation often struggles to simultaneously balance nutritional, physical, economic, and ergonomic targets due to isolated decision-making. This project transforms feed manufacturing from a static cost model into a dynamic, integrated factory system.  By utilizing a Lexicographic Fuzzy Goal Programming (LFGP) framework, the model aligns nutrition, manufacturing machine physics, production cost, and worker safety into a unified multi-objective optimization architecture across three broiler growth stages: Pre-Starter, Starter, and Finisher.  🎯 Key Objectives (The Lexicographic Hierarchy)The optimization model enforces four strict priorities in a preemptive sequence:  Nutritional Adequacy & Safety: Ensures all formulations strictly meet biological minimum and maximum bounds for key nutrients.  Pellet Durability Index (PDI): Maximizes physical feed quality (targeting a 92% PDI) using an empirical regression model of machine parameters.  Production Cost Minimization: Aggregates the cost of raw materials, steam energy, labor, and packaging into a single economic objective to be minimized.  Ergonomic Safety: Integrates the NIOSH Lifting Equation to compute the Recommended Weight Limit (RWL) and scale the Lifting Index (LI) for bagging operations.  🔬 Methodology & ArchitectureAnalytic Hierarchy Process (AHP): Validated the objective hierarchy using expert data analysis, confirming Nutritional Adequacy as the dominant criterion with a 55.8% priority weight.  Statistical Engineering (DoE): Executed a Two-Level, Four-Factor (2^4) Full Factorial Design in Minitab to map the non-linear thermodynamic interactions of temperature, moisture, particle size, and throughput on pellet durability.  Dynamic Shadow Pricing: Conducted parametric cost sweeps to stress-test the LFGP architecture against supply chain disruptions, allowing the algorithm to autonomously substitute overpriced ingredients without violating biological limits.  SLSQP Optimization: The 54-variable system was decrypted into three independent stage-by-stage optimization blocks and solved using a Sequential Least Squares Programming (SLSQP) optimizer.  📊 Key Results & Business ImpactFormulation Performance: Achieved high optimization satisfaction for pellet quality (94%) and nutritional adequacy (82%).  Cost Efficiency: Secured a moderate production cost optimization (71%) while successfully defending profit margins against simulated market volatility.  Ergonomic Diagnostics: Identified a critical safety violation in the baseline manual handling of 50 kg bags, which produced catastrophic NIOSH Lifting Index values ranging from 2.76 to 8.77.  CapEx Intervention & ROI: Modeled a semi-automated material handling retrofit (gravity conveyors and scissor lifts) that drops the Lifting Index to a safe 0.9. This intervention generates annual operational savings of ₹5,50,000, yielding a 122.2% Return on Investment (ROI) and a payback period of 0.81 years.  💻 Technologies & ToolsPython: Used for building the master LFGP non-linear solver and shadow pricing simulations (incorporating numpy, scipy.optimize.minimize, and matplotlib.pyplot).  Minitab: Utilized for the 2^4 full factorial experimental design and Pareto analysis.
